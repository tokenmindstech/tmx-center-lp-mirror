# Project conventions

- Copy style: never use em dashes anywhere in site copy. Use commas, colons, or periods instead. Section titles / headings must not end with a period.
- Account model: each TMX module (Visibility, UGC, KOL) has its OWN separate account and portal. There is no unified/shared TMX account. Never write copy implying one account works across modules. Present per-module accounts neutrally, not as a drawback.
- Portal URLs: Visibility https://visibility.tmx.center/login · UGC https://ugc.tmx.center/auth/login · KOL https://kol.tmx.center/login
- On the homepage, Login and Create Account actions open a small dropdown with the three module portals. On product pages they link directly to that product's portal.
- No "Try Platform" CTA anywhere.
